Privacy Policy — Wonder Egg World

Effective date: 22 June 2026 · A learning game for children ages 3–6.

Wonder Egg World is designed for children and is built to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, or share personal information. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do, in plain language.

1. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, microphone or camera data, or any other personal or identifying information. The app has no login, no account, and no chat.

2. Game progress stays on the device

Your child's progress (creatures collected, coins, decorations, settings) is saved only on the device using local storage. It never leaves the device, is not uploaded to us, and is removed if the app is uninstalled or progress is reset in the Parent Zone.

3. Advertising

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob. Because the app is directed to children, all ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalized (no behavioral profiling), rated G (general audiences). Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Parents can remove all ads permanently via a one-time in-app purchase.

4. Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive or store payment details. It is a one-time, non-consumable entitlement.

5. Analytics

If enabled, the app may use Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous, aggregate usage only (e.g. eggs hatched, daily return rate). These events contain no personal information. See Firebase privacy & security.

6. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

The app complies with COPPA, the EU GDPR for children, and Google Play's Designed for Families and Families Ads policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob, Play Billing, and Firebase if enabled), acting under their own policies for the limited purposes above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version at this URL.

9. Contact
