# Privacy Policy — Wonder Egg World

**Effective date:** 22 June 2026
**App:** Wonder Egg World (the "App"), a learning game for children ages 3–6.

Wonder Egg World is designed for children and is built to be **privacy-first**. We do
**not** collect, store, or share personal information. This policy explains exactly what
the App does and does not do, in plain language.

## 1. Information we do NOT collect
We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts,
microphone or camera data, or any other personal or identifying information. The App has
**no login, no account, and no chat**.

## 2. Game progress stays on the device
Your child's progress (creatures collected, coins, decorations, settings) is saved
**only on the device** using local browser/app storage (`localStorage`). It never leaves
the device, is not uploaded to us, and is removed if the App is uninstalled or progress is
reset in the Parent Zone.

## 3. Advertising
The free version of the App shows occasional full-screen ads from **Google AdMob**. Because
the App is directed to children, all ads are requested as **child-directed and
non-personalized** (no behavioral profiling), with content rated **G (general audiences)**:
- `tagForChildDirectedTreatment` = true
- `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent` = true
- non-personalized ads only (`npa` = true), `maxAdContentRating` = "G"

Google AdMob's handling of ad requests is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Parents can remove all ads
permanently via a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads").

## 4. Purchases
The optional **Remove Ads** purchase is processed by **Google Play Billing**. Payment is
handled entirely by Google; we never receive or store your payment details. The purchase is
a one-time, non-consumable entitlement tied to your Google account.

## 5. Analytics
If enabled in a given build, the App may use **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand
**anonymous, aggregate** usage (e.g. how many eggs are hatched, daily return rate). These
events contain **no personal information** — only anonymous game counters. See
[Firebase privacy & security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 6. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)
The App is intended for children under 13 and complies with the U.S. Children's Online
Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR for children, and Google Play's
**Designed for Families** and **Families Ads** policies. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request,
delete, or correct — but if you have any concern, contact us below.

## 7. Data sharing
We do **not** sell, rent, or share any data. The only third parties involved are Google
(AdMob, Play Billing, and Firebase, if enabled), acting under their own policies for the
limited purposes above.

## 8. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, we will update the "Effective date" above and post the new version
at the same URL.
